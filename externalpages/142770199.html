AIM Data Service 2.0 IntegrationThe next workstation QA build will have this plugin for testing.
Configuration of the AIM Data Service 2.0 plugin from within the ClearCanvas Workstation:
1. Click the Workstation's Tools menu -> Options -> AIM -> AIM Data Service 2.0
2. Enter in the AIM Data Service URL: http://imaging.cci.emory.edu/rest/aime-test/middleware/services/AIME/AIME4
3. Enter in the Secure Token Service Login URL: http://imaging.cci.emory.edu/rest/aime-test/middleware/securityTokenService
4. Click OK

Using the AIM Data Service 2.0 plugin
1. Click the Explorer tab within the Workstation
2. Click the AIM Data Service 2.0 tab
3. Enter search criteria in the search fields to narrow your search, or leave them blank to retrieve a list of all annotations
4. Click Search
5. Enter your user name and password for the Secure Token Service when presented with the log in dialog.
6. Click Log in.  The search will be performed and results displayed in the results pane at the bottom of the tab.
7. Click on an annotation from the list of results to select it
8. Click the Retrieve Selected Annotation button or right click a selected annotation and select Retrieve Selected Annotation from the context menu to download and import the annotation into the workstation.

AIM at University of Ibadan and University College Hospital

Dr. Mayowa Owolabi preforms imaging research with stroke patients. He and his team are defining a Case Report Form (CRF) to collect data for stroke patients. His team is translating the CRF to an electronic CRF or an AIM template in order to collect information in a structured manner. Dr. Mongkolwat spent time teaching a few members of his staff how to use AIM template builder (ATB, used to create an electronic CRF) and how to import the CRF to the AIM enabled workstation 3.5.0.1. The AIM workstation and AIM stroke template will be used to collect stroke patient data from eight other sites beside University College Hospital.
